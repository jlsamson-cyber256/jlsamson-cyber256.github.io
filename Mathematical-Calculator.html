Mathematical Calculator

Expression parser, calculus, algebra, number theory, statistics, base conversion. All arithmetic uses IEEE 754 double precision.

Angle unit

    Operators: + - * / ^ ! %. Implicit multiplication works (2pi, 3(4+1)). Assign variables with name=expression. Use ans for the last result. Functions: sin cos tan asin acos atan atan2 sinh cosh tanh ln log log2 exp sqrt cbrt root abs floor ceil round sign fact gamma nCr nPr gcd lcm mod min max hypot pow deg rad. Constants: pi, e, phi.

    

    Derivative: Richardson-extrapolated central difference. Integral: adaptive Simpson. Root: Newton iteration. Angle unit follows the Calculator tab.
