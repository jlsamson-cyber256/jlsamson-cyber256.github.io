Order of Operations Solver

Type an arithmetic expression. Every operation is carried out one at a time, in the correct order, and shown in succession.

Naming:

The rank table

RankPEMDASBODMAS
1ParenthesesBrackets
2ExponentsOrders (powers and roots)
3Multiplication and Division, left to rightDivision and Multiplication, left to right
4Addition and Subtraction, left to rightAddition and Subtraction, left to right

PEMDAS stands for Parentheses, Exponents, Multiplication, Division, Addition, Subtraction. BODMAS stands for Brackets, Orders, Division, Multiplication, Addition, Subtraction. They are two names for one rule, so both always give the same answer. Operations on the same rank run strictly left to right, except exponents, which run right to left (2^3^2 = 2^9). The label “BOMDAS” is not a standard acronym; BODMAS is the one to use.

Accepted input: numbers, + − × ÷ * / ^ **, and (), [], {} as grouping. A number touching a bracket, such as 2(3+4), is treated as ordinary multiplication at rank 3, not as a tighter bond. A minus sign applies after exponents, so −2^2 = −4.
